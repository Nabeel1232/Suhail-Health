# Simulated follow-up after a positive screening result

Simulated cohort under declared assumptions. Clinical outcomes were not measured.

In the simulated cohort, completion by day 360 rose from **56% to 69%** (scenario range 62.5%–75.4%). That corresponds to **1,300 additional completions per 10,000 actionable results** (scenario range 650–1,940).

| Simulated measure | Usual care | Suhail-supported navigation |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Colonoscopy by day 90 | 43.0% | 52.4% |
| Colonoscopy by day 180 | 51.0% | 63.9% |
| Colonoscopy by day 360 | 56.0% | 69.0% |
| No colonoscopy by day 300 | 45.0% | 32.0% |
| Unknown tracking or outcome at day 360 (proxy) | 44.0% | 0.0% |
| Median days among completers by day 360 | 33.67 | 48.86 |
| Modelled documented exceptions at day 360 | Not modelled | 31.0% |

The two medians summarize different groups of completers. Every paired case that completed in usual care retains its original completion date.
